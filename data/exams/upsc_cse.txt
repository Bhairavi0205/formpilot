EXAM: UPSC CSE (Civil Services Examination)
CONDUCTING BODY: Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
OFFICIAL WEBSITE: upsc.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 21-32 years (General category)
- Age Relaxation: OBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwD 10 years
- Maximum attempts: General 6, OBC 9, SC/ST unlimited till age limit
- Education: Bachelor's degree in any stream from recognized university
- Nationality: Indian citizen

EXAM PATTERN:
- Prelims: 2 papers - GS Paper 1 (200 marks) + CSAT Paper 2 (200 marks, qualifying)
- Mains: 9 papers - Essay, GS 1-4, Optional 1-2, Language papers
- Interview/Personality Test: 275 marks
- Total Mains + Interview: 1750 marks

SERVICES OFFERED:
- IAS (Indian Administrative Service)
- IPS (Indian Police Service)
- IFS (Indian Foreign Service)
- IRS (Indian Revenue Service)
- And 20+ other central services

SALARY: Rs. 56,100 to Rs. 2,50,000 per month

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FORM:
1. Aadhar Card - Identity proof, UIDAI centers, free
2. 10th Marksheet - DOB proof, from school/board
3. Graduation Certificate - Eligibility proof, from university
4. Category Certificate - SC/ST/OBC if applicable, Tehsildar office, 7-15 days, Rs. 50-100
5. Passport Size Photo - White background, 20-300 KB, 3.5cm x 4.5cm
6. Signature - White background, 20-300 KB
7. PwD Certificate - If applicable, from CMO office

PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm
- Background: White only
- File size: 20 KB to 300 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG

SIGNATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- File size: 20 KB to 300 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Black ink on white paper

APPLICATION FEE:
- General/OBC Male: Rs. 100
- Female/SC/ST/PwD: Exempt
- Payment: Online/SBI challan

IMPORTANT DATES (approximate - check upsc.gov.in):
- Notification: February
- Application Last Date: March
- Prelims Exam: May-June
- Mains Exam: September
- Interview: January-April next year
- Final Result: April-May next year

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Name must match exactly as per graduation certificate
- Optional subject selection is permanent - choose carefully
- Photo must be formal, white background
- Fill permanent and correspondence address carefully
- Category certificate must be from competent authority

ELIGIBILITY COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Can final year students apply?
A: Yes, final year students can apply for Prelims

Q: How many attempts are allowed for General category?
A: 6 attempts between age 21-32 years

Q: Is there any educational qualification for specific services?
A: No, any graduation stream is accepted for all services
