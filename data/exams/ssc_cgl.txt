EXAM: SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level)
CONDUCTING BODY: Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
OFFICIAL WEBSITE: ssc.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 18-32 years (General category)
- Age Relaxation: OBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwD 10 years
- Education: Bachelor's degree in any stream from recognized university
- Nationality: Indian citizen

EXAM PATTERN:
- Tier 1: Online MCQ - General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English (200 marks, 60 minutes)
- Tier 2: Online MCQ - Math, English, Statistics, General Studies (Finance) 
- Tier 3: Descriptive Paper - Essay/Letter writing (100 marks, 60 minutes)
- Tier 4: Computer Proficiency Test / Skill Test (qualifying)

POSTS OFFERED:
- Assistant Audit Officer (AAO)
- Assistant Accounts Officer
- Assistant Section Officer (CSS, MEA, SFIO, AFHQ)
- Inspector of Income Tax
- Inspector (Central Excise, Preventive Officer, Examiner)
- Sub Inspector (CBI, NIA)
- Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)
- Statistical Investigator Grade II

SALARY: Rs. 25,500 to Rs. 1,51,100 per month (Pay Level 4 to 8)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FORM:
1. Aadhar Card - Identity proof, available at UIDAI centers, free
2. 10th Marksheet - DOB proof, from your school/board, already have it
3. Graduation Certificate/Degree - Eligibility proof, from your college
4. Category Certificate - SC/ST/OBC certificate if applicable, from Tehsildar office, 7-15 days, Rs. 50-100
5. Passport Size Photo - White background, 20-50 KB, 3.5cm x 4.5cm
6. Signature - White background, 10-20 KB, 3.5cm x 1.5cm
7. PwD Certificate - If applicable, from government hospital

PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm
- Background: White only
- File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Recent photo (not more than 3 months old)

SIGNATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 3.5cm x 1.5cm  
- Background: White only
- File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Black ink on white paper

APPLICATION FEE:
- General/OBC: Rs. 100
- SC/ST/PwD/Female: Exempt (no fee)
- Payment: Online (debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI)

IMPORTANT DATES (approximate - check ssc.gov.in for exact dates):
- Notification: September-October
- Application Start: September-October  
- Application Last Date: October-November
- Tier 1 Exam: December-January
- Tier 2 Exam: March-April
- Result: 6-8 months after exam

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Name must match exactly as per 10th marksheet
- DOB must be in DD/MM/YYYY format
- Photo must be recent, white background only
- Signature must be on white paper with black pen
- Category must match your certificate exactly
- Email and mobile number must be active and correct

ELIGIBILITY COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Can final year students apply?
A: Yes, final year graduation students can apply provisionally

Q: Is there any age relaxation for ex-servicemen?
A: Yes, 3 years relaxation for ex-servicemen

Q: Which graduation stream is required?
A: Any stream - BA, BCom, BSc, BCA, BBA all accepted
