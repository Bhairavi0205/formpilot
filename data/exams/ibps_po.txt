EXAM: IBPS PO (Probationary Officer)
CONDUCTING BODY: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection
OFFICIAL WEBSITE: ibps.in

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 20-30 years (General category)
- Age Relaxation: OBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwD 10 years
- Education: Bachelor's degree in any stream
- Computer literacy required
- Nationality: Indian citizen

EXAM PATTERN:
- Prelims: English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning (100 marks, 60 minutes)
- Mains: Reasoning & Computer, English, Data Analysis, General Economy/Banking (200 marks, 3 hours)
- Mains English Descriptive: Letter + Essay (25 marks, 30 minutes)
- Interview: 100 marks
- Final: Mains 80% + Interview 20%

BANKS COVERED:
- Bank of Baroda
- Bank of India
- Bank of Maharashtra
- Canara Bank
- Central Bank of India
- Indian Bank
- Indian Overseas Bank
- Punjab National Bank
- Punjab & Sind Bank
- UCO Bank
- Union Bank of India

SALARY: Rs. 36,000 to Rs. 52,000 per month (with allowances Rs. 52,000+)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FORM:
1. Aadhar Card - Identity proof, free
2. 10th Marksheet - DOB proof
3. Graduation Certificate - Eligibility proof
4. Category Certificate - SC/ST/OBC if applicable, Tehsildar
5. Passport Size Photo - White/light background, 20-50 KB, 4.5cm x 3.5cm
6. Signature - White background, 10-20 KB
7. Left Thumb Impression - Scanned, 20-50 KB
8. Hand Written Declaration - Scanned copy

PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 4.5cm x 3.5cm (height x width)
- Background: White or light colored
- File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG

SIGNATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Black/blue ink

THUMB IMPRESSION:
- File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Left thumb, black/blue ink pad

APPLICATION FEE:
- General/OBC/EWS: Rs. 850
- SC/ST/PwD: Rs. 175
- Payment: Online only

IMPORTANT DATES (approximate - check ibps.in):
- Notification: August
- Application: August-September
- Prelims: October
- Mains: November
- Interview: January-February
- Final Result: April

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Photo size is different from other exams - 4.5x3.5 not 3.5x4.5
- Thumb impression must be clear and complete
- Handwritten declaration must be in own handwriting
- Check bank preference carefully
- Graduation percentage must be correct

ELIGIBILITY COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Is there minimum percentage in graduation required?
A: No minimum percentage specified by IBPS

Q: Can final year students apply?
A: Yes, provisionally. Degree required at time of joining.

Q: Can I apply for both IBPS PO and IBPS Clerk?
A: Yes, both are separate exams, apply separately
