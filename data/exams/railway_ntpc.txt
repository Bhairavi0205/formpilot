EXAM: RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories)
CONDUCTING BODY: Railway Recruitment Board (RRB)
OFFICIAL WEBSITE: indianrailways.gov.in, rrbcdg.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 18-30 years (General category) - varies by post
- Age Relaxation: OBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwD 10 years
- Education: 12th pass (Junior Clerk, Accounts Clerk) OR Graduation (Station Master, Goods Guard)
- Nationality: Indian citizen

EXAM PATTERN:
- CBT 1: General Awareness, Math, General Intelligence (100 questions, 90 minutes)
- CBT 2: General Awareness, Math, General Intelligence (120 questions, 90 minutes)
- Typing Skill Test / CBAT (for specific posts)
- Document Verification

POSTS OFFERED:
- Junior Clerk cum Typist
- Accounts Clerk cum Typist
- Junior Time Keeper
- Trains Clerk
- Commercial cum Ticket Clerk
- Station Master
- Goods Guard
- Senior Commercial cum Ticket Clerk

SALARY: Rs. 19,900 to Rs. 35,400 per month

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FORM:
1. Aadhar Card - Identity proof, UIDAI centers, free
2. 10th Marksheet - DOB proof, from school
3. 12th Marksheet or Graduation - Eligibility proof, from college
4. Category Certificate - SC/ST/OBC if applicable, Tehsildar, 7-15 days, Rs. 50-100
5. Passport Size Photo - White background, 20-50 KB
6. Signature - White background, 10-40 KB
7. EWS Certificate - If applicable, Tehsildar office

PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm
- Background: White only
- File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG

SIGNATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- File size: 10 KB to 40 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Black ink on white paper

APPLICATION FEE:
- General/OBC: Rs. 500 (Rs. 400 refunded after CBT 1)
- SC/ST/PwD/Female/Minority/EBC: Rs. 250 (Rs. 250 refunded after CBT 1)
- Payment: Online only

IMPORTANT DATES (approximate - check rrbcdg.gov.in):
- Notification: Once in 2-3 years (large recruitment)
- Application: 30 days window
- CBT 1: 3-4 months after application
- CBT 2: 2-3 months after CBT 1
- Final Result: 12-18 months after notification

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Select correct RRB board for your region
- Post preference order is very important
- Photo must be recent, plain white background
- Name spelling must match 10th certificate exactly
- Mobile number must be active for 2 years

ELIGIBILITY COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Can 12th pass students apply for all posts?
A: No, some posts require graduation. Check post-wise eligibility carefully.

Q: Is there negative marking?
A: Yes, 1/3 negative marking in CBT 1 and CBT 2

Q: Can I apply for multiple RRB regions?
A: No, only one RRB region allowed per candidate
