EXAM: MPSC Rajyaseva (Maharashtra Public Service Commission)
CONDUCTING BODY: Maharashtra Public Service Commission
OFFICIAL WEBSITE: mpsc.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 19-38 years (General category)
- Age Relaxation: OBC/SBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwD 10 years
- Education: Bachelor's degree from recognized university
- Domicile: Maharashtra domicile required for most posts
- Language: Marathi language knowledge required

EXAM PATTERN:
- Prelims: GS Paper 1 + CSAT Paper 2 (qualifying) - 400 marks
- Mains: 6 papers - Marathi, English, GS 1-4 - 1100 marks
- Interview: 100 marks
- Total: 1200 marks

POSTS OFFERED:
- Deputy Collector
- Deputy SP (Police)
- Assistant Commissioner Sales Tax
- Assistant Commissioner State Excise
- Tehsildar
- Block Development Officer
- Child Development Project Officer

SALARY: Rs. 41,800 to Rs. 1,32,300 per month

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FORM:
1. Aadhar Card - Identity proof, UIDAI centers, free
2. 10th Marksheet - DOB proof
3. Graduation Certificate - Eligibility proof
4. Domicile Certificate - Maharashtra resident proof, Tehsildar office, 7-15 days, Rs. 20-50
5. Caste Certificate - SC/ST/OBC/SBC if applicable, Tehsildar, Rs. 50-100
6. Caste Validity Certificate - Required for reserved category, Caste Scrutiny Committee
7. Non-Creamy Layer Certificate - For OBC candidates, Tehsildar, valid 1 year
8. Passport Size Photo - White background, 20-50 KB
9. Signature - White background, 10-20 KB

PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm
- Background: White only
- File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG

SIGNATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG

APPLICATION FEE:
- General: Rs. 524
- OBC/SBC: Rs. 324
- SC/ST/PwD: Rs. 224
- Payment: Online only

IMPORTANT DATES (approximate - check mpsc.gov.in):
- Notification: February-March
- Application Last Date: March-April
- Prelims: May-June
- Mains: November-December
- Interview: March-April next year

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Domicile certificate must be of Maharashtra
- Caste validity certificate is compulsory for reserved category
- Non-creamy layer certificate must be within 1 year
- Marathi medium preferred for answer writing
- Name must match across all documents

ELIGIBILITY COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Is Maharashtra domicile compulsory?
A: Yes for most posts, domicile certificate required

Q: Which language for Mains exam?
A: Marathi is compulsory. English paper also there.

Q: Is NCL certificate required every year?
A: Yes, Non-Creamy Layer certificate must be renewed yearly
