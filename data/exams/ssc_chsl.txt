EXAM: SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level)
CONDUCTING BODY: Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
OFFICIAL WEBSITE: ssc.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 18-27 years (General category)
- Age Relaxation: OBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwD 10 years
- Education: 12th pass (Higher Secondary) from recognized board
- Nationality: Indian citizen

EXAM PATTERN:
- Tier 1: Online MCQ - General Intelligence, English, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness (200 marks, 60 minutes)
- Tier 2: 
  Part A - MCQ Math + Reasoning + English + General Awareness
  Part B - Skill Test/Typing Test (qualifying)

POSTS OFFERED:
- Lower Division Clerk (LDC)
- Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)
- Postal Assistant (PA)
- Sorting Assistant (SA)
- Data Entry Operator (DEO)
- DEO Grade A

SALARY: Rs. 19,900 to Rs. 25,500 per month

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FORM:
1. Aadhar Card - Identity proof, free
2. 10th Marksheet - DOB proof, from school
3. 12th Marksheet - Eligibility proof, from school/board
4. Category Certificate - SC/ST/OBC if applicable, Tehsildar, Rs. 50-100
5. Passport Size Photo - White background, 20-50 KB, 3.5cm x 4.5cm
6. Signature - White background, 10-20 KB

PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm
- Background: White only
- File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG

SIGNATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
- Format: JPG/JPEG
- Black ink on white paper

APPLICATION FEE:
- General/OBC: Rs. 100
- SC/ST/PwD/Female: Exempt
- Payment: Online only

IMPORTANT DATES (approximate - check ssc.gov.in):
- Notification: November-December
- Application: December-January
- Tier 1 Exam: March-April
- Tier 2 Exam: June-July
- Result: 4-6 months after exam

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Only 12th pass required - do not write graduation details
- Typing speed requirement - 35 wpm English, 30 wpm Hindi
- Name must match 10th marksheet exactly
- Age calculation as on 1st January of exam year

ELIGIBILITY COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Can graduation students apply for CHSL?
A: Yes, graduates can apply but post preference limited

Q: What typing speed is required?
A: 35 words per minute English or 30 words per minute Hindi

Q: Is there any sectional cutoff?
A: No sectional cutoff in Tier 1, only overall cutoff
